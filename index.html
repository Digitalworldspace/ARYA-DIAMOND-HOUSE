What actually makes a diamond

Every stone we set is graded on the same four measures, mined or grown.

Cut

How precisely the facets are angled to catch and return light. The single biggest driver of sparkle.

Color

Graded D–Z by how colorless the stone is. We set almost everything D–G.

Clarity

The presence, or absence, of natural inclusions formed during crystal growth.

Carat

The stone's weight, not its size — two diamonds of equal carat can look different depending on cut.

Grown, then cut by hand

Each rough stone is grown over several weeks using CVD or HPHT methods, replicating the pressure and temperature deep underground. Once it reaches size, it's sent to our cutting bench, faceted, polished and independently graded before it ever touches a setting.

Every finished piece leaves with its lab certificate, so you know exactly what you're wearing.
